# The closing call: discovery → offer → close

The Worker emails you a **pre-call brief** the moment someone books. It covers who they are, the likely pain, the best demo angle, four discovery questions, objections and a suggested pilot shape. If they filled in the thank-you page form, you also get the question their AI gets wrong today. Read both before you join.

The call is 30 minutes on video.

## 0. Frame (1 min)
"Thanks for booking. Here's how I'd like to use the 30 minutes: I'll ask about what you're running and where it breaks, then show you cited retrieval on content like yours. At the end we'll decide together whether a pilot makes sense. If it doesn't, I'll tell you. Sound good?"

## 1. Discovery (10 min)
Work from the brief's four questions. Fill these gaps:
1. **Sources:** what content, how much, what formats (documents, recordings, video)?
2. **Consumers:** which agents, apps or people use the answers, and through what (MCP client, own app)?
3. **Failure:** the last time an answer was wrong or couldn't be sourced. What happened, and who noticed?
4. **Constraints:** jurisdiction, security review, on-prem, model restrictions.
5. **Cost today:** what retrieval costs now (infra, re-indexing, engineering time).
6. **Decision:** who else signs off, what would they need to see, and by when?

Summarize back in one sentence: "So if knowdrive gave you X for Y, by Z, that would be worth doing?"

## 2. Demo (8 min)
Use the brief's **best demo angle**. In the Inspector, show one cited result with the file, version and byte range, then a media result returning a timestamp. If they sent their "question your AI gets wrong", run its closest analogue. Keep it to two queries and one wow moment.

## 3. Offer (5 min)
- **Pilot:** a store provisioned in their jurisdiction, a representative slice of their corpus, wired into one agent or app, measured against their current stack on cost per query, answer quality and citation coverage.
- **Commercials:** Enterprise from $5,120/month. That's a flat org fee plus compute at cost, with storage overage per GB. A deployment package and enablement days are included, and further support is billed at standard day rates.
- **Timeline:** a concrete start date and success criteria written down.

## 4. Close (5 min)
- "Based on what you told me, does a pilot on those terms make sense?"
- **If yes:** agree the start date, the success criteria and who signs. Send the order form the same day and mark the lead **Won** in the admin with the first-year value. That fires the Purchase signal to the ad platforms.
- **If "need to think":** ask what specifically. Is it budget, timing, a stakeholder or technical doubt? Solve it on the call or book the follow-up with that stakeholder before you hang up.
- **If no:** thank them and mark **Lost**. If they're early, point them to the free plan and leave the door open.

## After the call
Mark the stage in the admin (Showed, Won or Lost) within the hour. **Showed** and **Won** are sent back to Meta, LinkedIn and Google as conversions. Those are the signals that make targeting improve over time.
