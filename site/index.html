The problem

Your agents answer fast. Nobody can check where the answer came from.

Most teams run a vector database, a file store and a chunking pipeline that were built separately. Costs climb with every re-index, answers drift, and when legal or a customer asks "where did that come from?" there is no trail.

knowdrive replaces those three systems with one store. Content is vectorized once and read at any scale, every result carries its citation, and your material is isolated per store, encrypted at rest and never used to train models.

Good fit

  • You ship retrieval, search or agents to customers or staff
  • You need an audit trail for what your AI tells people
  • Your data has to stay in Canada, the EU or on your own hardware
  • Retrieval cost or answer quality is already a line item

Better served by the free plan

  • Personal projects and early prototypes
  • Teams without a production use case this year

Questions

Before you apply

What does it cost?

Enterprise plans start at $5,120/month: a flat org fee plus compute at cost, with storage overage billed per GB. Self-serve plans start free (500 MB, 500 searches a month) if you want to try it first.

Where does our data live?

In the jurisdiction you choose. knowdrive runs on ordinary Arm CPUs, so it can run in a Canadian or EU region or on your own hardware. Each store is isolated and encrypted at rest, and nothing is used to train models.

Which models and tools does it work with?

Any MCP-compatible assistant (Claude, Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, Cursor and others) plus a REST API for your own applications. Search is vector, full-text or hybrid across 80+ languages.

What happens after I apply?

If the application looks like a fit you pick a time straight away. If it doesn't, we send you the free plan and the resources that match what you told us, so you are never left waiting.

What do I get from the call?

A scoped view of your sources and constraints, a live look at cited retrieval on content like yours, and a written go/no-go with what a pilot would cost.
