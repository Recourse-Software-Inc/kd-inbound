What changes for your team

“Where did this come from?” has an answer

Every result points to the unchanged source: file, version, and byte range or timestamp. Your record stays the record you're accountable for.

Change models without rebuilding

Your content is vectorized once. Move between Claude, GPT or a local open model and nothing gets re-chunked or re-embedded.

Lower cost per answer

Agents pull the exact passage they need, so the context window goes to reasoning and smaller models handle work that used to need the biggest ones. Runs on ordinary CPUs rather than GPUs.

Data stays where you're accountable for it

In your jurisdiction or on your own hardware. Each store is isolated, encrypted at rest and never used to train models.
